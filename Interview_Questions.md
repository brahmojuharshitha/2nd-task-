# Task 2 — Interview Questions (Preparation)

1. **What is the importance of data visualization?**  
   It turns data into charts that make patterns, comparisons, and unusual values easier to understand.

2. **When do you use a pie chart vs. a bar chart?**  
   Use a pie chart for a small number of parts of a whole. Use a bar chart to compare values across categories.

3. **How do you make visualizations more engaging?**  
   Use a clear title, readable labels, suitable chart types, limited colors, and a short takeaway.

4. **What is data storytelling?**  
   It is explaining what the data shows, why it matters, and what action could be taken.

5. **How do you avoid misleading visualizations?**  
   Use correct scales, clear units, relevant time periods, accurate labels, and do not hide important context.

6. **What are best practices in dashboard design?**  
   Keep the layout simple, group related charts, show important KPIs first, use consistent formatting, and avoid clutter.

7. **What tools have you used for visualization?**  
   Suggested honest response: “For this practice task, I used the provided CSV and created a visual report. I am also learning how to recreate the charts in Power BI Desktop.”
